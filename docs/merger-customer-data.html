Combining customer data after a merger

Use this when two companies merge and their customer databases need to become one.

Steps

  1. Check the legal basis first: can customers of company A be contacted by company B? Involve data protection early.
  2. Map both data models: which fields exist, what they mean, which system is the master.
  3. Define matching rules for duplicates (name, email, tax ID) and test them on a sample.
  4. Agree with both sales teams who owns shared customers before the data is merged.
  5. Run a trial merge on a copy, review errors, then migrate in waves.
  6. Inform customers where the law requires it and document what was done.

Common pitfalls

  • Merging first and asking legal later.
  • Letting one side's team feel their data was 'overwritten'.
